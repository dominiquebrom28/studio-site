## Learnings

- **The camera found a bug on night one.** The fastest validation of a
  hire the studio could have asked for: the exact class of defect that
  motivated the role (visible-only-in-browser) turned up in the first
  hour of its existence, in the boss's own site.
- **Honest media is a discipline, not a default.** Every shortcut in the
  capture pipeline (virtual time, palette crushing, cropping the nav bug,
  faking motion) was cheaper than the honest path; the house rules are
  what made the deliverable trustworthy. Same lesson as writing, new
  medium.
- **Names change review, not just voice.** Reviewing Sanne's draft as
  "did Sanne overclaim?" made two guardrail violations (an unsourced
  number, a self-contradicting closing line) jump out in a way generic
  copy-editing hadn't on earlier posts. The characters may be for
  readers, but they sharpen the editors too.

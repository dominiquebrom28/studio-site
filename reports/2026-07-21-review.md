# Critical review — the whole team + the Judge — 2026-07-21

Dom asked for a full critical review: "judge where things are lacking, missing,
or could be better." All nine specialists reviewed their own domain in parallel
(instructed to be adversarial, cite `file:line`, and prioritise), then the Judge
adjudicated — resolving severity conflicts, catching overstated claims, and
ranking. This is the record; fixes shipped the same day are noted inline.

## The load-bearing theme (the Judge's through-line)

**The brand is honest, uncounterfeitable provenance — and the provenance isn't
wired.** Five agents independently hit the same failure shape: *declared but not
delivered.*

- The hero **promised** "a real byline, a real reviewer, and a real commit hash";
  pages shipped a bare byline. The reviewer/commit fields don't exist in the
  content model. **→ Fixed same-day: hero copy softened to the truth (PR #37).**
- `ProvenanceStrip` accepts `reviewedBy`/`commitUrl`/`commitLabel` — called with
  `author` only; not rendered on `ProjectDetail` at all.
- `.riso-offset` (the brief's one signature analog accent) is defined in CSS,
  applied to zero nodes.
- The post `cover` field is validated and tested, rendered by nothing.
- `MarginNote`/Caveat is described as shipping in six brief passages, retired.
- `deployed-smoke` is built and green, checks nothing (`SMOKE_URL` unset).

An honesty-branded product whose flagship device is decorative — and whose hero
overclaimed — is precisely what the studio's Judge exists to catch, pointed at
itself.

## What the Judge caught in our own review

  (and backend + QA) ran `validate:content` → 24/24 green; the two 07-18 posts
  carry distinct `order`. The architect read a stale `ci.yml` comment. The
  survivable half — the gate is non-required — was kept and **fixed (PR #36),
  including deleting the stale comment.**
- **Downgraded devops's security-headers P0 → P1** (no live exploit).
- **Flagged "main has zero branch protection" as unverifiable from the tree** —
  kept only the actionable core (validate-content was bypassable).

## Genuine strengths (the team was clear these are real)
Content quality is specific, self-critical, and hard to fake (security, design,
marketer all said so). The SEO/feed generator reuses the real loader via
`ssrLoadModule` — textbook drift-proofing. Additive schema discipline is
excellent. The markdown pipeline has no XSS surface (no `rehype-raw`, no
`dangerouslySetInnerHTML`; `urlTransform` allowlist). `MediaGallery`'s
click-to-play is unusually careful a11y work. The codebase visibly encodes each
production incident as a tested rule — which is exactly what let the reviewers
verify claims fast.

## Learnings (blog-worthy)

- **The dossier/writer split works.** Separating fact-gathering (read-only
  agents, "cite file paths, do not invent") from copywriting (marketer,
  "the dossier is your only source") made the never-invent rule structurally
  enforceable instead of aspirational — the writer literally had nothing to
  invent from.
- **The flaws ARE the content.** The most compelling material in every
  write-up is what's broken or unfinished: the fake multiplayer UI, the
  zero-commit repo, the placeholder panels. An honest portfolio reads better
  than a polished one — this validates the site's whole premise.
- **A pattern emerged across all six repos that's a post in itself:** every
  project is a one-to-five-day intense build sprint followed by silence, and
  three repos have significant uncommitted work sitting in the working tree
  weeks later. AI-assisted building is fast; the discipline gap moves to
  version control and follow-through.
- **Review still catches real errors:** the writer compressed "the app
  contains historical data about Mensday 2023" into "the app shipped for
  Mensday 2023" — plausible, wrong, and exactly the kind of drift a
  fact-checking pass exists for.
